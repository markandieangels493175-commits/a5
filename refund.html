Refund Policy

Clear information about how Warm Foot Works operates.

Before purchase

This site is primarily an editorial and enquiry resource. An enquiry does not create an order. A paid product or service proposal will state price, size, materials, timing and cancellation terms before payment.

Standard goods

For eligible standard goods sold at a distance, cancellation and return rights will be explained at checkout in line with applicable law. Returned goods should meet the stated period and hygiene conditions, and not be used beyond reasonable inspection.

Hygiene and personalised items

Worn hosiery may have return limitations for hygiene reasons, and personalised items may have limited cancellation rights after work begins. Any limitation will be disclosed before purchase without restricting statutory rights.

Faults and refunds

Statutory rights for faulty, damaged or misdescribed goods remain. Email hello@warmfootworks.com with the order reference and helpful photographs. Approved refunds are returned to the original payment method; provider processing times vary.

Questions may be sent to hello@warmfootworks.com or by post to 3638 Westbourne Grove, London, UK, W2 5SH, GB.
