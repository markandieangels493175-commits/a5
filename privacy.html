Privacy Policy

Clear information about how Warm Foot Works operates.

Information we receive

When you use the contact form, we receive the name, email address, selected topic and message you choose to provide. We use it to answer your enquiry and maintain an appropriate correspondence record.

Analytics and consent

Google Analytics is configured with consent mode. Analytics storage is denied by default and enabled only if you choose “Allow analytics.” Advertising storage, ad user data and ad personalisation remain denied.

Use and retention

We use information to operate the site, respond to messages, maintain security and meet legal duties. We do not sell personal information. Limited providers may process data under appropriate instructions. Records are retained only as reasonably necessary.

Your choices

Declining analytics does not restrict site access. To request access, correction or deletion of information linked to an enquiry, email hello@warmfootworks.com. Legal exceptions may apply.

Questions may be sent to hello@warmfootworks.com or by post to 3638 Westbourne Grove, London, UK, W2 5SH, GB.
