Shipping Policy

Clear information about how Warm Foot Works operates.

Availability and dispatch

No product is immediately available unless confirmed during an order. Dispatch estimates, carrier choices and charges are supplied before payment. Small-batch timelines may vary.

Address and tracking

Provide a complete delivery address. Tracking is sent where available after dispatch. Carrier scans and estimated dates can change and are not guaranteed delivery appointments.

International delivery

International orders may incur tax, duty, customs processing or carrier fees. Responsibility for charges will be explained before purchase where known; border processing may extend delivery time.

Transit problems

Inspect the parcel promptly. If damaged, retain packaging, photograph the condition and email hello@warmfootworks.com as soon as practical. We will review evidence and applicable carrier processes without limiting statutory rights.

Questions may be sent to hello@warmfootworks.com or by post to 3638 Westbourne Grove, London, UK, W2 5SH, GB.
